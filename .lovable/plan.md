# Everlasting Flower Bouquet Chapter

## Goal
Add a romantic bouquet page to the birthday journey so the flowers remain as a permanent digital gift.

## Experience
- Place the bouquet after the letter archive and before the quiet finale.
- Present a handcrafted arrangement of softly glowing lilies and romantic blooms within the existing night-sky style.
- Reveal the bouquet with a gentle bloom animation and the message “Flowers for you, so they never die.”
- Include one clear control to continue to the final chapter.

## Implementation
- Add the bouquet as a new scene in the existing chapter sequence and update the chapter indicator and progress.
- Build the bouquet with lightweight HTML/CSS shapes, preserving reduced-motion support and phone layouts.
- Update both the live birthday project and the downloadable single-file `birthday-story.html`.
- Keep all existing wishes, letters, personal messages, music, and interactions unchanged.

## Validation
- Play through from the letter archive to the bouquet and finale.
- Check desktop and phone layouts, no sideways scrolling, controls, and current build health.
